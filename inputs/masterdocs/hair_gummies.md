## Persona
# Target Customer Persona: Early-Stage Hairfall, 18–26

**Who they are:** Young Indian men, 18–26, in college / first job / hostel / PG / mess-hall living. Urban, often mid-transition — a new city, a new job, a new routine — which is exactly when their diet quality drops hardest.

**Key pain points:** Hairfall has visibly started — strands on the comb, on the pillow, in their hands while shampooing, in the shower drain. They've already tried the usual home remedies (egg on scalp, onion paste, vitamin-E capsules, dahi, kadhi patta, methi), multiple oils (coconut, almond, amla, mustard), and anti-dandruff/anti-hairfall shampoos — and biotin/multivitamin tablets — with nothing working. They don't understand the actual cause. Many fall into "the waiting trap": blaming water, weather, or a city change, and waiting 2–8 months while hairfall quietly advances — a real, factual risk, not just anxiety.

**Motivations:** They want the *real* reason explained — not another home remedy, not vague reassurance — and a fix that's simple, daily, science-backed, and fits an 18–26-year-old's actual life (mess food, quick meals, irregular timings). They respond to proof (93% result stat), consistency framing (90-day expectation, not overnight), and ingredient transparency (naming Biotin/Zinc/Vitamins A,C,E specifically, explaining what each does).

**How they speak about their problem:** Hinglish, casual, deflecting: *"Paani kharab hai," "sheher change hua," "mausam hai"* — external excuses for an internal cause. Once they understand the real reason: *"Ohhh, so THAT'S why my hair is falling"* / *"Yaar, mai bhi yahi galti kar raha tha — internal nutrition ke baare mein toh socha hi nahi"* / *"Ohhh, mera khaana hi hairfall ki wajah ho sakta hai — aur mai wait karke time waste kar raha hu."*

## Product Brief
# Man Matters Hair Gummies: Product Brief

## What It Is
A daily nutritional supplement addressing early-stage hairfall in men 18–26 by fixing the actual root cause — a nutrition gap that typical Indian diets (mess food, quick meals, irregular timings, hostel/PG living) don't fill — rather than treating symptoms from the outside like oils and shampoos do.

## Composition & Attributes
Biotin (Vitamin B7) · Zinc · Vitamin A · Vitamin C · Vitamin E. Strawberry flavour, no added sugar, gelatin-free, gluten-free, gut-friendly, 100% vegan.

## Key Ingredients and Their Roles
- **Biotin (Vitamin B7):** Strengthens hair structure from within; reduces breakage and shedding; helps fix the nutritional gap that causes hair to fall in the first place.
- **Zinc:** Repairs damaged hair follicles from within.
- **Vitamins A, C & E:** Support healthy scalp circulation; help nutrients reach hair roots more effectively over time.

## Usage
1 gummy daily, after any meal — morning or night, the person's own call. Not a replacement for a balanced diet — gummies bridge the nutritional gap that diet alone doesn't cover; maintaining a proper diet alongside is still important.

## Primary Benefits / Result Claim
93% of men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days. Requires consistent 90-day use for visible results — the messaging must never overpromise faster timelines.

## Proof Points
- 93% of men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days
- Clinically tested ingredients
- Doctor-backed, dermatologist-approved formulations
- Trusted by 10 lakh+ (1 million+) men — India's largest men's wellness brand

## Differentiators
Addresses hairfall from the inside (nutrition) rather than the outside (oils/shampoos, which coat the hair shaft and scalp surface but never reach follicle level). Positioned as the nutrition-first entry point in the Man Matters hair range — built to stop hairfall before it gets worse, not to reverse or regrow.

## Approved Claims / What To Avoid (source of truth for Copy Health scoring)

**USE THESE:**
- "93% men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days"
- "Clinically tested ingredients"
- "Doctor-backed / dermatologist-approved"
- "10 lakh+ men trust Man Matters"
- "Reduces hairfall caused by nutritional deficiency"

- "Designed for early-stage hairfall"

**AVOID THESE — hard compliance boundary, not stylistic preference:**
- "Regrowth" / "new hair" guarantees
- "Cures baldness" / "reverses balding"
- "Stops thinning" / "baal patle nahi honge" — this product is hairfall-only, not a thinning/regrowth product
- "100% results" / "guaranteed in X days"
- Naming or trashing competitor brands
- Medical claims ("treats", "medicine", "cures")
- Promising results without the 90-day consistency framing

## Narrative
# Man Matters Hair Gummies: Narrative Analysis (3 creative briefs, shared shoot backbone)

## Core Story Arc

All three narrative variants share one locked 8-scene shoot backbone: (1) Hook — hard hairfall visuals (comb/pillow/hands/drain) → (2) What You Tried — every wrong remedy shown failing (nuske, oils, shampoos, biotin tablets) → (3) The Waiting Trap — "Log 2 se 8 mahine wait karte hain, itna risk kyun lena?" → (4) The Real Reason — the nutrition gap, explained simply, not as a lecture → (5) The Fix — Hair Gummies introduced on camera, ingredients explained (cover at least 2 of 3: Biotin/Zinc/Vitamins) → (6) Consistency, 90 Days — mandatory montage of the person eating the gummy in different outfits/settings → (7) Before/After — mirrors scene 1's visuals, now clean → (8) CTA.

Universal non-negotiables across all three: every 3 seconds must earn continued attention (B-roll, jump cuts, text, sound FX — never a static talking-head video); background music throughout, never silent; hairfall visuals are mandatory, not optional; every wrong remedy must be shown failing on screen with a "didn't work" stamp; packaging/gummy box/single-gummy close-ups must be shown clean and well-lit; 60–90 seconds, vertical 9:16, English + Hinglish captions; the 90-day expectation must be stated — never overpromise.

## Narrative 1 — Problem → Solution (the default/lead narrative)
**Big idea:** Hairfall has started, every home remedy failed, the real unnamed cause is a nutrition gap, the fix is simple and starts now.
**What to shoot:** Open on hairfall recognition (18–26 age framing), show the failed remedies, name the waiting-trap risk explicitly, pivot to explaining *why* Indian meals fail to deliver Biotin/Zinc/Vitamins A,C,E, introduce the product with ingredient explanation, close on 90-day consistency + clean before/after.
**Target emotional landing line:** *"Ohhh, so THAT'S why my hair is falling — and that's exactly what hair gummies is preventing."*

## Narrative 2 — Myth-Busting (lean-in: nuske/oils/shampoos ≠ the fix)
**Big idea:** Same opening problem, but the narrative role is explicitly a call-out — every wrong remedy gets busted on camera with a "DIDN'T WORK" stamp, reframing hairfall as an internal nutrition problem that external products can never fix.
**Truth-flip pivot line (verbatim, in-language):** *"Yeh sab bahar se kar rahe ho — hairfall andar se ho raha hai. Ek nutrition gap hai jo mess food, quick meals, irregular diet se hota hai. Biotin, Zinc, Vitamins — yeh diet se mil hi nahi rahe. Aur jab follicle ko nutrition nahi milta — hairfall start ho jata hai."*
**Target emotional landing line:** *"Yaar, mai bhi yahi galti kar raha tha — internal nutrition ke baare mein toh socha hi nahi."*

## Narrative 3 — Nutrition (lean-in: 18–26 Indian diets ≠ enough hair nutrition)
**Big idea:** Heaviest on relatable food/lifestyle B-roll — the real culprit is the person's own everyday diet (mess food, Swiggy, Maggi, skipped breakfast, late-night snacks), not something external.
**Key line:** *"Indian diets generally lack these specific hair nutrients. It's not your fault — but it is your problem."* and *"This is not magic. Diet bhi maintain karna padega. Gummy bridge karta hai woh gap jo regular meals se cover nahi hota."*
**Mandatory adds unique to this narrative:** show the person's actual eating reality on camera (mess plate / delivery app / late-night snacking); extra emphasis, spoken slowly with text overlay, on the "2 to 8 months waiting" insight; ingredients framed as gap-fillers, not the headline — the headline is the diet problem itself.
**Target emotional landing line:** *"Ohhh, mera khaana hi hairfall ki wajah ho sakta hai — aur mai wait karke time waste kar raha hu."*

## Key Narrative Pillars (all 3 variants share these)
1. **Visible problem recognition** — hairfall shown through relatable, everyday moments.
2. **Failed traditional solutions** — home remedies, oils, shampoos positioned as ineffective distractions (not mocked — the audience tried them in good faith).
3. **The hidden truth** — a nutrition gap in Indian diets as the overlooked root cause.
4. **Science-backed simplicity** — one daily gummy delivers what diet alone can't, without replacing a proper diet.

## Emotional Journey
Anxiety (seeing hairfall everywhere) → frustration (nothing worked) → relief (finally understanding why) → empowerment (a simple, consistent daily fix exists). The "waiting trap" (blaming water/weather/city) is deliberately reframed as active risk, not passive anxiety.
