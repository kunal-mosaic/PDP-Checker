## Product Brief
# Product Brief: Advanced Hair Regrowth Regime

## What It Is
The Advanced Hair Regrowth Regime is a three-part hair loss treatment system designed for men with male pattern baldness. The kit includes Minoxifin Advance topical solution, Biotin Gummies, and a 0.5mm Dermaroller with 540 titanium alloy micro-needles.

## Key Ingredients and Their Roles
**Minoxifin Advance** combines 5% Minoxidil (increases blood flow to follicles) with 0.1% Finasteride (blocks DHT, the primary hormonal cause of hair loss), delivered via patented CETOSOMAL® technology—an alcohol-free lipid carrier with hydrating agents.

**Biotin Gummies** provide Biotin for keratin production, Zinc for tissue repair, and Vitamins A, C, and E for scalp nourishment, collagen synthesis, and improved circulation.

**Dermaroller** creates micro-channels in the scalp to activate dormant follicles and enhance topical absorption.

## Primary Benefits
The regime works through synchronized triple-action: topical follicular delivery, internal systemic support, and mechanical channel creation. The alcohol-free formula eliminates scalp dryness, redness, and itching common with standard minoxidil products, improving daily adherence.
