## Persona
# Target Customer Persona: Tejas

**Who They Are**

Tejas is a married man, typically a business owner or senior professional in a high-output, client-facing role. He lives in Tier 1 or large Tier 2 Indian cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, or Indore, earning ₹10–25 lakhs annually. He travels frequently for work—often 15 days a month—and family is central to his identity. He's a consistent gym-goer (3–5 times weekly), but fitness is infrastructure, not identity. He's already taking supplements like whey protein, ashwagandha, or magnesium.

**Age Range**

34–45 years old, with the sweet spot being 36–42. Old enough to feel aging, young enough to fight it.

**Key Pain Points**

His main struggle isn't the gym—it's surviving the workday. The 10 AM to 7 PM window drains him: meetings, travel, client pressure. His current supplement stack doesn't touch those hours. He's running on "gym residue and caffeine." He also quietly battles visible aging—hair loss, white hairs, declining energy—though he won't name this fear directly. He needs formats that travel well; anything requiring preparation gets abandoned.

**Motivations**

Tejas wants dignity, not abs. His core driver: "Daughter should feel proud—yes, he is my father." He doesn't want to be the tired dad at the back of the school hall. He's fighting decline, not chasing transformation.

**How He Speaks About His Problem**

"Gym toh kar raha hu, but between the gym and getting home, the day just grinds me down."
"Energy boost chahiye, work ki vajah se exhaust ho jate hai."
"Science toh samajh nahi aata utna."

He's practical, not clinical—he trusts his trainer and gym friends over research.

## Product Brief
# Shilajit Gummies Product Brief

## What It Is

Shilajit Gummies are a convenient, vegetarian supplement designed to support cellular energy, vitality, and recovery. Delivered as a ready-to-eat gummy, the product eliminates the preparation hassles of traditional shilajit resin while providing consistent dosing and superior absorption.

## Key Ingredients and Their Roles

The formula centres on **Shilajit** (300mg per serving, standardised to 60% fulvic acid), which supports cellular energy by carrying nutrients into cells. **KSM-66 Ashwagandha**, the world's most clinically studied ashwagandha extract, reduces cortisol and aids recovery. **Black Musli** provides sustained energy throughout the day, while **Black Pepper** (piperine) enhances bioavailability of all ingredients. **Chicory Root** serves as a natural prebiotic sweetener with a glycaemic index of just 2.

## Primary Benefits

The gummies deliver improved fitness performance, reduced stress hormones, enhanced nutrient absorption, and sustained daily energy—without acidity or sleep disruption.

## Proof Points

Man Matters conducted two independent 90-day, randomised, placebo-controlled clinical trials published in peer-reviewed journals. Results showed a 25% improvement in fitness score (VO₂ max-linked) and 23% reduction in cortisol levels. Every batch undergoes third-party lab testing for purity, heavy metals, and fulvic acid content.

## Differentiators

The proprietary **ShilAbsorb™ technology** delivers 2.5x better bioavailability than raw resin. The product contains no added sugar, artificial sweeteners, chemicals, or preservatives. Unlike messy, bitter resin requiring daily preparation, these travel-ready gummies support consistent habit formation.

## Narrative
# Shilajit Gummies: Narrative Analysis

## Core Story Arc

The central narrative positions Shilajit Gummies as the modern solution for the responsible adult male who does everything right but still hits a wall. Whether he's a gym-goer, desk worker, or fitness enthusiast, the story follows the same trajectory: a man managing multiple responsibilities—career, family, health—finds that effort alone isn't enough to sustain him through the day. The 3 PM crash becomes the universal antagonist, and the gummy format emerges as the frictionless, trustworthy answer.

## Key Narrative Pillars

Three pillars anchor the messaging: **sustained energy** (solving the afternoon crash without stimulant spikes), **clean formulation** (no added sugar, no chemicals, naturally sweetened with chicory root), and **modern convenience** (gummy format versus traditional resin preparation). A fourth supporting pillar—**verified safety**—reinforces credibility through third-party lab testing and published clinical studies.

## Emotional Journey

The emotional arc moves from quiet frustration to confident control. The problem isn't framed as failure but as an unmet need: "I'm doing everything right, so why am I still drained?" This validates the consumer before introducing the solution. The resolution delivers autonomy—showing up fully for family, staying sharp at work, pushing one more rep—without requiring lifestyle overhaul.
