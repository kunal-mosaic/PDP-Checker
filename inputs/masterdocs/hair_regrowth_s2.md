Hair ICP – Stage 2 vs Stage 3
Based on: 272 S2 male responses + 306 S3 male responses + 213 customer call verbatims + 60 NPS responses
1. Who They Are
|  | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Age | 18–26 yrs (67% of base). Students and working professionals. | 22–34 yrs. Working professionals dominate. |
| Occupation | Students ~45%, Private sector ~30%, Engineers ~9% | Private sector ~40%, Students ~28%, Business/freelance ~12% |
| Hair condition | Frontal recession, mild thinning. 54% reduced density. 30% are actually preventive (S1). | Crown + frontal loss visible. 78% reduced density. 7% are actually S4. Bald patches present. |
| Emotional distress | 6.8/10. Fear-driven but manageable. | 7.2/10. Higher distress. Crown loss harder to hide. |
| Doctor visit rate | 25% visited a dermat before MM. | 41% visited a dermat before MM. More clinical-minded. |
| Kit purchase | Partial kit / BG-only common. Gummies as entry hook. | 61% buy full kit. 31% buy partial/BG-only (high churn risk). |

One-Line ICP
• S2: 18–26 yr old student/young professional who spots frontal recession, sees a relatable
Instagram ad, gets his friend’s confirmation, and buys hoping to stop losing what he has.
Will panic at shedding in week 3 and quit before month 2 if no one tells him it’s normal.
• S3: 22–34 yr old working professional with visible crown + frontal loss, who has already
tried Thriveco/Traya/home remedies and been disappointed. Researches on Gemini +
YouTube + reviews. Buys the full kit hoping for bald-patch regrowth. Will silently churn in
month 2–3 if doctor doesn’t deliver a specific milestone roadmap.
2. What They’re Actually Buying

<!-- page 3 -->

|  | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Core motivation | PRESERVATION. “Jitne hai utne bachana chahta hoon.” Insurance against S3. | REVERSAL. “Bald patches pe baal wapas chahiye.” Get back what’s already gone. |
| What they expect | Hairfall stops. What I have stays. Quick signal in 4–6 weeks. | Baby hair on bald spots. Visible regrowth. Specific month-by-month milestones from the doctor. |
| Messaging that works | “Baal bachao” not “baal ugao.” “Abhi shuru nahi kiya toh Stage 3 tak pahunch jaoge.” | “Bald areas mein wapas aana.” Month 4–6 crown recovery journey. Before/after on bald patches. |

3. What They Were Doing Before MM
| Pre-MM cluster | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Did nothing | 43% – first-time treaters. Ignored until mirror shock or mummy noticed. | 31% – ignored for 1.5–2 years. External trigger (wedding, job, mirror) finally broke the pattern. |
| Oils / home remedies | 16%. Coconut, onion, castor oil. “Kuch nahi hua 6 months mein.” | 18%. More varieties tried for longer. Desperate attempts including “secret website oils.” |
| OTC shampoos | 12%. Clinic Plus, H&S, Pilgrim. Dandruff relief only. | 14%. Same. Shampoo-only approach = known failure for S3. |
| D2C brand switcher | 14%. Plix, Thriveco, Pilgrim, Traya. “Thriveco ka koi result nahi.” | 22% – HIGHEST cluster. Brand-burned and skeptical. Traya: “too complex.” Thriveco: “no result.” |
| Dermat / Rx | 8%. Local dermat, generic minox. Moved online for convenience. | 10%. PRP tried and failed. Generic minox tried. MM brand trust > pharmacy generic. |
| Key insight | Most are clinical first-timers. Low research, impulse + peer nudge. | Most have tried and failed at least once. High research, skeptical, need more proof. |


<!-- page 4 -->

4. How They Find Us + Convert
|  | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| #1 trigger | Relatable Instagram/YT ad (37%). “Ad mein mera same situation tha.” | Friend/family result evidence (35%). Must SEE visible result on friends, not just recommendation. |
| #2 trigger | Friend/roommate recommendation (32%). “Dost ne mangwaya. 4 log mere circle mein use karte hain.” | Relatable ad + reviews loop (35%). Ad alone not enough. Needs comments section, reviews validation. |
| #3 trigger | Mirror shock / visible hairfall (20%). Photo or video call reveals hairline. | Full protocol promise (25%). “MM ka full system tha – shampoo, spray, gummies, roller, doctor.” |
| Conversion journey | SHORT. 1 relatable ad + 1 friend’s nod = purchase. Single-step works. | LONG. Ad → YouTube research → Gemini/ChatGPT comparison → reviews → friend proof → doctor promise. |
| Research depth | Low. Instagram ad + friend. Price check, not ingredient check. | High. Compares MM vs Traya on Gemini. YouTube journey videos month 3–6. Reads comment sections. |
| Primary channel | Instagram ads (discovery + conversion). | Instagram (discovery) + YouTube (research) + Gemini/ChatGPT (validation) + Friend result (closer). |
| Competitor threat | Bare Anatomy (“cheaper + 4 week claim”). | Traya (“more clinical, more thorough”). Transplant as parallel option for advanced S3. |

5. Purchase Drivers
Functional
| Driver | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary goal | Stop hairfall (65%). “Save what I have.” | Regrowth on bald patches (70%). “Reverse what’s gone.” |


<!-- page 5 -->

| Doctor credibility | 55%. Free consult = #1 reason for choosing MM. | 60%. Doctor 41%. NPS promoters: “Doctor consultation helpful” = top reason. |
|---|---|---|
| Safety / tested | 48%. “Too many chemicals” = #1 put-off. Needs reassurance. | 45%. Already past chemical anxiety. Wants proof of safety, not avoidance. |
| Simple routine | 30%. “Traya ka login tough. MM ka simple – Do hi products.” | Less relevant. 61% buy full kit. Accepts complexity if it works. |
| Price point | 28%. Budget ₹500–1K (50%). ₹900 = low barrier. | 25%. Budget ₹500–1K (56%). Cost anxiety grows over 6-month protocol. |

Emotional
| Driver | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence | 70%. #1 across all S2 data. | 72%. #1 across all S3 data. Higher distress (7.2 vs 6.8/10). |
| Social judgment | 45%. “Side se udde huwe lagte hai.” Cap to hide hairline. | 45%. Crown loss harder to hide. Every cap-removal = anxiety event. |
| Dating / career | 40%. Presentability, interview anxiety, dating confidence. | 22% dating, 30% “look young.” Crown loss = age-marker. Career image acute. |
| Genetic anxiety | 20%. “Papa ka tha.” Knows where it’s going. | 10%. Less about genetics, more about reversing visible loss now. |

6. Barriers & Frictions
| Barrier | Stage 2 | Stage 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Results too slow | 55%. #1 frustration. “Results take too long” = 29/94. Most in shedding phase – education failure, not product failure. | 60%. #1 frustration. “No growth on bald spots after 3 months.” Bald patch regrowth needs 4–6 months. 58% rate 1–2/5. |


<!-- page 6 -->

| Usage confusion | 40%. Every cohort. “Minox baalo pe ya scalp pe?” 38% dose correctly, 21% overdose, 15% underdose. | Equally high. Kit more complex: product sequencing, dermaroller timing, shampoo-before-minox not communicated. |
|---|---|---|
| Shedding panic | 35%. Week 2–6 hairfall not warned. “Baal kyu jhadenge?” = product failure in user’s mind. | 30%. Even more alarming at S3 – already anxious. Additional shedding without warning = catastrophic. |
| Routine gaps | 30%. “Forgot to apply.” Travel, dirty hair, winter night application. | 28%. More holistically engaged but still struggles with daily routine. |
| Cost | 22%. “Bahut mahanga 6 months ke liye.” Budget ₹500–1K. | 25%. Longer treatment + more products. Cost anxiety grows without visible results. |
| Side effects fear | 20%. “Minoxidil se addiction hoga.” Mostly pre-use, resolves with doctor. | Lower. Already decided on medication. Fear = long-term dependency, not initial safety. |
| Doctor gap | 12%. “Free call band ho gayi after bulk order.” | 25%. “Doctor timeline baar baar badha deta hai.” Inconsistent quality = trust collapse. |
| Transplant exit | Not relevant. | 8%. S3+ not seeing results research transplants. Hard to reactivate. |

7. Three Strategic Truths Per Stage
Stage 2
• 1. They buy insurance, not regrowth. “Jitne hai utne bachana.” “Stage 2 se Stage 3 nahi
jaana.” But MM ads lead with regrowth (S3 creative). Structural messaging misalignment
at scale.
• 2. Shedding phase is the #1 self-inflicted retention crisis. ~55% of detractors cite slow
results or unexpected hairfall. Not a product failure – zero pre-communication. Every
dropout generates negative WoM that kills the referral engine (32% of S2 comes from
friends).
• 3. Usage confusion is the most predictable churn accelerator. Present in every cohort
regardless of age, city, tier. Minox dosage: 38% correct, 21% overdose, 15% underdose. A
Day-1 WhatsApp video eliminates this entirely.

<!-- page 7 -->

Stage 3
• 1. They want regrowth, not preservation. Opposite of S2. Bald patches visible. “Baby
hair on bald areas by month 4–5” is the right creative. Doctor must set a credible
milestone at first consult.
• 2. One ad is not enough – they’re deliberate researchers. They’ve been burned before.
Gemini, YouTube, comment sections. Full-funnel required: TOF ad → MOF comparison
content → BOF friend proof + doctor promise.
• 3. Doctor consult sets expectations – if it fails, they churn. Needs: specific
month-by-month milestones, shedding warning, mandatory Month 3 check-in. “Doctor
baar baar timeline badha deta hai – I don’t trust this.” Quality inconsistent across panel.
8. So What – Next Quarter Priorities
Messaging & Creative
• S2 creative: Preservation-first. “Abhi shuru nahi kiya toh Stage 3 tak pahunch jaoge.” Cap
removal shock. Week-by-week hairfall reduction. Stop showing crown recovery in S2 ads.
• S3 creative: Bald-patch reversal. Month 4–6 crown area journey. Dedicated S3 ad set, not
shared with S2. “Meri tarah jo Stage 3 mein unke liye yeh hai.”
• Comparison content: S3 buyers compare on Gemini/ChatGPT. Own this: blog, YouTube,
structured data. Price + simplicity + doctor = our angle vs Traya.
• Competitor framing: Bare Anatomy = S2 threat. Traya = S3 threat. Different responses
per stage.
Onboarding & Retention
• Day 1 WhatsApp video: 60-sec application demo + shedding phase warning.
Non-negotiable for S2 + S3.
• S2 Month 2 auto-trigger: Doctor check-in. Highest churn moment. Usage audit +
shedding status + expectations reset.
• S3 Month 3 auto-trigger: Doctor check-in. Bald patch regrowth starts month 3–4.
Catches them before they quit.
• Prescription on WhatsApp: Within 30 min of every consult. Currently missing for many
users.
Doctor Consult
• S2: Pre-empt shedding. 6-week hairfall reduction milestone. Hindi explanation for Tier
2/3.
• S3: Mandatory milestone map: Week 4–6 hairfall slows → Month 2–3 density up →
→
Month 4–5 baby hair on crown Month 6 visible regrowth. This = retention anchor.

<!-- page 8 -->

• Standardise: “Traya ka doctor bahut descriptive tha – MM ka doctor time nahi deta.” Fix
the inconsistency. Make milestone map mandatory output from every consult.
• Decouple from order: Tie consult to 6-month usage window, not order quantity.
Product & Protocol
• S2 upsell: Gummies = entry. Post-consult upsell to full protocol (minox + gummy +
shampoo).
• S3 BG-only rescue: 31% buy partial – guaranteed detractors. Month 1 outreach:
“Gummies alone won’t restore bald patches.”
• Stage misclassification: 30% of S2 = actually S1. 35% of S3 = closer to S2. 7% of S3 =
actually S4. Doctor must flag and adjust.
• Cetosomal upgrade: Advanced layer positioned as “doctor-recommended upgrade”
during consult. Key AOV lever.
Acquisition
• S2: Instagram-first. Single ad + friend referral. Vernacular Hindi for Tier 2/3. Peer referral
program (32% comes from friends).
• S3: Full funnel. TOF ad → MOF YouTube/blog comparison → BOF friend proof + doctor
promise. Single-step doesn’t work.
• S3 YouTube: Month 3–6 crown journey content = the S3 closer. Invest in S3-specific
journey creators.

7. Three Strategic Truths Per Stage
Stage 2
• 1. They buy insurance, not regrowth. “Jitne hai utne bachana.” “Stage 2 se Stage 3 nahi
jaana.” But MM ads lead with regrowth (S3 creative). Structural messaging misalignment
at scale.

results or unexpected hairfall. Not a product failure – zero pre-communication. Every
dropout generates negative WoM that kills the referral engine (32% of S2 comes from
friends).
• 3. Usage confusion is the most predictable churn accelerator. Present in every cohort
regardless of age, city, tier. Minox dosage: 38% correct, 21% overdose, 15% underdose. A
Day-1 WhatsApp video eliminates this entirely.

Stage 3
• 1. They want regrowth, not preservation. Opposite of S2. Bald patches visible. “Baby
