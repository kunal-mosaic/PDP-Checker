## Persona
# Target Customer Persona: Man Matters Stage 1 Anti-Hairfall Kit

**Who They Are**

Young Indian men experiencing early-stage hair loss—noticing thinning hair, increased shedding on their comb, pillow, or shower drain. They're likely urban, digitally native, and consume content primarily through short-form vertical video on platforms like Instagram and YouTube. They're educated enough to appreciate science-backed explanations but want information delivered simply and quickly.

**Age Range**

Early 20s to mid-30s—young enough that hair loss feels premature and alarming, but not yet at a stage requiring medical intervention like transplants.

**Key Pain Points**

They've tried everything their family recommended—eggs, onion paste, coconut oil, various shampoos—and nothing worked. They've wasted months waiting, hoping the problem would resolve itself, blaming external factors like water quality, weather, or a city change. They're confused about what's actually causing the problem and frustrated by generic advice that doesn't address root causes. There's underlying anxiety about their hairline receding further while they hesitate.

**Motivations**

They want to stop hairfall before it progresses to something irreversible. They're looking for a solution that feels credible—science-backed, not another "ghar ka nuska." They want visible proof and a clear, simple routine they can stick to.

**How They Speak About Their Problem**

Hinglish, casual, self-aware: *"Baal patle ho rahe hain." "Tried everything—oil, shampoo, onion paste—kuch kaam nahi kiya." "Log 2 se 8 mahine wait karte hain—aur tab tak hairline peeche jaa chuki hoti hai."* They deflect with excuses ("paani kharab hai, mausam hai") but privately worry they're running out of time.
