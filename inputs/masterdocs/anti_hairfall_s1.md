## Product Brief
# Man Matters Stage 1 Anti-Hairfall Kit — Product Brief

## What It Is
A science-backed, dual-action system for early-stage hairfall and hair thinning — internal nutrition (Hair Gummies) plus external strengthening (Advanced Hair Serum), addressing the two root causes simultaneously: DHT and a nutrition gap. Positioned as the flagship for early-stage hairfall/thinning, built to intervene before a transplant conversation ever becomes necessary.

## The Two Root Causes (must always be explained together, not vaguely)
- **DHT hormone** — shrinks follicles, causing new hair to grow in thinner ("follicles ko shrink karta hai, naya baal patla aata hai").
- **Nutrition gap** — daily food doesn't fully deliver the Biotin, Zinc, Vitamin A/C/E the follicles need.
- Lifestyle, stress, and sleep are secondary contributing triggers, mentioned but not the headline.

## Key Ingredients and Their Roles

**ANDAR SE — Hair Gummies (internal nutrition layer):**
- **Biotin (Vitamin B7):** Strengthens hair structure, reduces breakage and shedding.
- **Zinc:** Repairs damaged follicles from within.
- **Vitamins A, C & E:** Help nutrients reach hair roots more effectively over time.

**BAHAR SE — Advanced Hair Serum (DHT + strength layer):**
- **3% Procapil:** Blocks DHT at the follicle.
- **3% Redensyl:** Works on hair follicle stem cells, reactivating them — clinically tested.
- **Anagain** (listed alongside Procapil/Redensyl in product quick facts).
- **1% Rosemary:** Improves scalp circulation.

**The science line to land on camera:** *"Andar se nutrition, bahar se DHT block — yeh hai woh actual science jo nuske mein nahi hai"* (nutrition from inside, DHT-blocking from outside — this is the actual science that home remedies don't have).

## Usage
1 gummy after any meal (strawberry flavour, no added sugar, gelatin-free, gluten-free, gut-friendly). Serum: 1ml on scalp once daily, morning or night, water-based and non-sticky.

## Primary Benefits / Result Claim
93% of men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days with consistent use. Positioned strictly as: stops hairfall, reduces thinning, strengthens weak hair — **never** as regrowth or increased hair density (explicitly forbidden in creative direction).

## Proof Points
93% of men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days; clinically tested ingredients; dermat-recommended; trusted by 10 lakh+ Indian men; natural ingredients that are clinically proven.

## Approved Claims / What To Avoid (source of truth for Copy Health scoring)

**USE THESE:**
- "93% men saw reduced hairfall in 90 days"
- "Clinically tested ingredients"
- "Dermat-recommended"
- "10 lakh+ men trust Man Matters"

## Differentiators
Dual-action (internal + external, not single-lever like oils/shampoos); natural, clinically proven ingredients; formulated specifically for men's biology; convenient format (gummy + non-sticky serum); positioned as early intervention before more invasive treatment is ever needed.
