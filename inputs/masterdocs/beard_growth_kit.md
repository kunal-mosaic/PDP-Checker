##### 11. Communication Rules
###### What to do
Keep the tone natural, modern, visual, and native to Reels/TikTok-style content.
Show the kit as a simple aesthetic daily routine for young men.
Use product close-ups throughout: Minoxidil application, gummies, derma roller, shelf shot, hand texture, mirror routine.
Use music-led, fast-paced edits with text overlays.
Explain science with clean ingredient callouts, not heavy medical lecture.
Repeat 3-4 months consistency. This is the core behaviour.
Use peer proof, real reviews, before-after, and month markers.
Use doctor-backed proof for older or cautious users.
###### What not to do
Do not make the viewer feel bad about his face, masculinity, or age.
Do not use pressure, shame, or comparison to insult the customer.
Do not overpromise results.
Do not make the product sound like a grooming gimmick.
Do not turn every video into a heavy explainer.
Do not lead with jargon unless the audience is the research-led Patcher persona.
